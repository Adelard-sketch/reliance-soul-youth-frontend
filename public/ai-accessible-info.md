# Reliance Soul International Youth Foundation - AI-Accessible Information

## Organization Overview
**Name:** Reliance Soul International Youth Foundation (RSYI)  
**Type:** Non-Governmental Organization (NGO)  
**Founded:** 2020  
**Location:** Uganda, East Africa  
**Website:** https://www.reliancesoulfdn.org

## Mission & Vision
**Mission:** To empower young refugees and marginalized individuals through arts, cultural expression, vocational skills, and psychosocial support, fostering healing, leadership, and sustainable livelihoods.

**Vision:** Reached, Touched, and Changed marginalized souls for freedom and self-reliance.

## Core Values
- **Creativity** - We innovate and inspire through art
- **Empathy** - We listen, connect, and uplift
- **Integrity** - We act with honesty and purpose
- **Community** - We grow stronger together
- **Resilience** - We turn challenges into strength
